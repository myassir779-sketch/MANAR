# Free services register

No external service is activated for core functionality in Phase 1. The optional Google Fonts stylesheet is a network dependency, not a translation service.

Potential future hosting and model options must be reviewed at the time of adoption. “Free tier” does not necessarily mean unlimited, permanent, commercially usable, or free of payment-card requirements. Record the exact plan and date checked in `COST_POLICY.md` and `COST_REVIEW.md` before activation.
